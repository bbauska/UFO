# The Five Observables

Military and intelligence data often categorize unexplained encounters using five distinct flight characteristics 
defined by groups like "To The Stars":
<ul>
  <li>Sudden Acceleration: Rapid maneuvers with G-forces that exceed biological limits or current manufacturing physics.</li>
  <li>Hypersonic Velocities: Traveling at extreme speeds without a sonic boom, heat signature, or exhaust.</li>
  <li>Low Observability: Appearing opaque or semi-metallic while eluding clear electro-optical or radar locking.</li>
  <li>Transmedium Travel: Moving smoothly between air, space, or water.</li>
  <li>Positive Lift: Staying aloft without traditional wings, rotors, or propulsion surfaces.</li>
</ul>
